## Implementation Overview

F013 adds online, recipient-bound workspace member invitations with explicit approved access, authoritative database expiry, single-use acceptance, resend rotation, revocation, and delivery truth separate from membership. It enforces:

- **Online Session & Active Workspace Context**: Administration and redemption require live F010 verified session, enrolled device, and F012 active workspace context; no offline outbox or cached membership authority can invite or accept.
- **Opaque Approved Access Offers**: Invitations accept only approved `offerRef` values defined by current workspace policy; caller-composed permissions, arbitrary capability lists, and unapproved roles are rejected.
- **Strict Recipient Binding**: Binding uses either an established stable identity `(issuer, subject)` or an approved verified claim digest; display names, email strings, and contact records never grant membership authority.
- **Private Authority Separation**: Policy, invitation, acceptance-generation, delivery-attempt, and short-lived redemption-attempt tables remain private with no direct Data API exposure. `public.workspace_memberships` receives only accepted active members with full provenance.
- **Platform Cryptography & Zero Secret Leakage**: Random 256-bit secrets and lookup selectors are minted in memory; only domain-separated digests are persisted. Raw secrets, tokens, provider payloads, and secret-bearing URLs are never sent to desktop, logged, or exposed.
- **Single-Use Acceptance & Invalidation**: Each invitation has at most one current generation. Resend supersedes earlier generations before dispatching a new one; revocation terminalizes all generations; acceptance locks invitation, generation, and membership atomically to create exactly one active membership.
- **Delivery Truth**: Creation, queued attempt, provider acceptance, delivery, review, acceptance, and active membership remain distinct operational states. Provider failure never rolls back a committed invitation or revives old links.
- **Neutral Public Disclosure**: Signed-out, wrong-account, expired, or non-existent invitation links collapse to neutral safe unavailable states without disclosing workspace or recipient existence.
- **Bilingual Accessibility**: Full English and Arabic parity, authentic Arabic terminology, `<bdi>` bidi text isolation, keyboard-only operation, visible focus traps, non-color semantic states, and 200% zoom reflow.

## Implementation Entry Decisions and Gates

1. **Verification of Prerequisites**:
   - **F009 (Authoritative Receipts & Audit Evidence)**: Verified; F013 commands reuse F009 operation claim, semantic digest validation, receipt recording, and immutable audit projections.
   - **F010/F011 (Verified Session & Enrolled Device)**: Verified; invitation administration requires live non-anonymous session, active profile, and enrolled device check. Hosted redemption verifies live session before review.
   - **F012 (Active Workspace Context & Fencing)**: Verified; main coordinator derives current workspace envelope and selection generation; mutations fail closed when workspace is inactive or stale.

2. **Disposition of Open Release & Governance Gates**:
   - **Q-05 (Inviter Authority & Assignable Offer Profiles)**: Open gate. Production invitation policies are disabled (`enabled = false`). Synthetic policy `f013_v1_synthetic` is used exclusively for testing.
   - **Q-08 (Expiry, Resend & Identity Change Policies)**: Open gate. Database time governs expiry; production expiry windows remain unratified and synthetic defaults are used for testing.
   - **Q-02 (Sender, Domain, Provider Quotas & Abuse Handling)**: Open gate. Real email dispatch and external webhooks remain disabled; synthetic delivery sink captures attempts without external side effects.
   - **Q-13 (PII & Provider-Reference Retention)**: Open gate. Raw provider payloads, headers, and secrets are excluded from persistence; only redacted operational digests and allowlisted reason codes are retained.
   - **Production Delivery Status**: Public delivery and production offers remain blocked. Feature entry is permitted under synthetic testing and local database verification.

## Delivery Architecture Breakdown

- **Shared Contracts**: Versioned exact-key DTOs, request/response types, lifecycle enums, and failure contracts in `app/shared/invitation-contracts.ts`.
- **Database Schema & RPCs**: Migration `supabase/migrations/20261023000013_f013_member_invitations.sql` with private tables, partial unique indexes, immutable revisions, and restricted functions (`GetInvitationPolicy`, `CreateInvitation`, `ResendInvitation`, `RevokeInvitation`, `ListInvitations`, `ExchangeInvitationSecret`, `ReviewInvitation`, `AcceptInvitation`, `ReconcileInvitationOperation`).
- **Main Coordinator & Services**: `app/main/invitations/invitation-service.ts` and `app/main/invitations/coordinator.ts` managing profile/workspace generation fencing, serialization, and safe error mapping.
- **Preload & Bridge**: Narrow typed IPC methods (`invitation.getPolicy`, `invitation.list`, `invitation.create`, `invitation.resend`, `invitation.revoke`, `invitation.reconcile`) in `app/preload/preload.ts` and `app/main/bridge.ts`.
- **Renderer UI & Accessibility**: `InviteMemberDialog.tsx` and `InvitationStatusList.tsx` in `app/renderer/components/workspaces/` with full English/Arabic parity and bidi isolation.
- **Hosted Redemption**: `web/auth/invitation.tsx` handling fragment secret exchange, HttpOnly continuation cookie, verified-recipient review, and single-use acceptance.

## Operations, Rollback & Secret Redaction Guidance

- **Zero-Secret Boundary**: Raw 256-bit secrets, continuation tokens, Auth session credentials, raw recipient binding keys, and provider payloads must never cross the Electron bridge, enter logs, or appear in test output.
- **Rollback Behavior**: If F013 database functions or routes are revoked, existing invitations, history, accepted memberships, and audit evidence remain intact. Rollback disables entry points without dropping tables or deleting accepted members.
- **Partition & State Integrity**: Acceptance updates `workspace_memberships` additively with accepted invitation provenance; membership removal remains out of scope (F014).
- **Support Route**: When invitation delivery fails or is unknown, administrators follow safe reconciliation and explicit resend rather than guessing delivery or membership.
