## Success Criteria Dispositions (SC-001 through SC-013)

- **SC-001 (Verified Enrollment & Acknowledgment)**: **PASSED**. Tested in T020 and T021. In 100% of tests, setup completes only after explicit saved-code acknowledgment. Cancellation or expiry leaves prior state unchanged.
- **SC-002 (MFA Challenge Fail-Closed Enforcement)**: **PASSED**. Tested in T028 and T029. Replayed, cross-account, stale-generation, or offline challenge attempts produce zero authority.
- **SC-003 (Single Effect Atomicity)**: **PASSED**. Tested in T020 and T043. Every accepted intent produces exactly one generation increment and one evidence record.
- **SC-004 (Recovery Code Consumption & Rotation)**: **PASSED**. Tested in T034. Unused codes succeed at most once; rotation invalidates all prior unused codes.
- **SC-005 (Non-Enumeration Neutrality)**: **PASSED**. Tested in T030 and T035. Existing and non-existing accounts return identical public responses and timing tolerance.
- **SC-006 (Session Consequences & Stale Rejection)**: **PASSED**. Tested in T042 and T043. Stale operations are rejected; session consequences are explicitly tracked.
- **SC-007 (Local Key Limitation & Data Retention)**: **PASSED**. Tested in T036. Cloud identity recovery emits `local_unlock_required` when the local key is missing and never overwrites encrypted local bytes.
- **SC-008 (Secret Redaction & Canary Scan)**: **PASSED**. Prohibited secret canaries are rejected by validators and never appear in DTOs, evidence, or IPC.
- **SC-009, SC-010, SC-011 (Usability & Comprehension)**: **SIMULATED PASS; PENDING PARTICIPANT EXECUTION**. Protocol defined in `docs/qa/F011-usability-protocol.md`. Execution with external human participants remains gated on sandbox provisioning.
- **SC-012 (Bilingual Meaning Parity & Accessibility)**: **PASSED**. Tested in T022, T049, T050, and T051. 100% parity across English and Arabic dictionaries.
- **SC-013 (Honest Next Action on Failure)**: **PASSED**. Tested in T049, T056, and T057. Every failure state emits an honest next action (`retry`, `recover`, `sign_in_again`, `unlock_local`).
